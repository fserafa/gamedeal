What about gamedeal.ga?

gamedeal.ga is a streamlined hub designed to bring you the best gaming discounts and free games from across the web. Originally inspired by community-driven sharing on Reddit, it strips away the clutter and noise to present you with a clean, fast, and modern interface. Our goal is to make saving money on games as effortless and distraction-free as possible, transforming standard deal tracking into a premium user experience.
